# Kestrel Color Bulb B2 User Manual

## Specifications

- Base: E26 (A19 shape)
- Brightness: 800 lumens (60 W equivalent), 9 W actual power
- Color: 16 million colors, tunable white 2000K to 6500K
- Connectivity: Zigbee 3.0 via Kestrel Hub H1, plus Bluetooth for setup without a hub
- Rated life: 25,000 hours
- Not for use in fully enclosed fixtures or with dimmer switches.

## Setup

1. Screw the bulb into a lamp or fixture controlled by a regular on/off switch and turn it on. The bulb flashes once to show it's ready.
2. In the Kestrel Home app tap Add Device > Light.
3. With a hub, the bulb joins your Zigbee network. Without a hub, it connects over Bluetooth and works only when your phone is within about 30 feet.

## Using with wall switches and dimmers

Keep the wall switch on. If the switch is turned off, the bulb loses power and can't be controlled from the app or schedules.

Don't install the B2 on a circuit with a dimmer switch. A dimmer causes flickering, buzzing, and can damage the bulb. Set the dimmer to 100% and leave it there, or replace it with a regular switch.

## Power-on behavior

By default the bulb turns on at warm white 2700K, 100% brightness when power is restored. You can change this in Light Settings > Power-on behavior to "Last state."

## Scenes and color

Create scenes under Rooms > Scenes. Each scene can store color and brightness for up to 50 lights. Transition time can be set from 0 to 10 seconds.

## As a repeater

When connected to a hub, the B2 extends the Zigbee and Thread mesh for other Kestrel devices. Keep it powered (wall switch on) so the mesh stays healthy.

## Troubleshooting

**Bulb flickers.** Usually a dimmer on the circuit, a loose bulb, or a fixture with an illuminated switch. Check for a dimmer first.

**Bulb shows "No response."** The wall switch is probably off. Turn it on and wait 30 seconds.

**Bulb won't pair.** Reset the bulb (see below) and keep it within 30 feet of the hub during pairing.

**Colors look off.** Re-run calibration from Light Settings > Color calibration.

## Reset

Turn the light switch off and on 5 times in a row: on for about 1 second, off for about 1 second. On the 5th time the bulb flashes 3 times to confirm the reset.
