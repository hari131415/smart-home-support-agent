# Kestrel Video Doorbell D2 User Manual

## Specifications

- Video: 1536p HD, 160° diagonal field of view, HDR, infrared night vision up to 20 feet
- Power: hardwired 16-24 VAC (10 VA minimum transformer) or rechargeable 6000 mAh battery
- Wi-Fi: 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, minimum 2 Mbps upload per doorbell
- Weather rating: IP65, operating temperature -4°F to 122°F (-20°C to 50°C)
- Storage: cloud recording with Kestrel Protect plan; 30-day video history on the Plus plan

## Battery and charging

A full charge typically lasts 2 to 4 months depending on the number of events. Charge with the included USB-C cable; a full charge takes about 5 hours using a 5V/2A adapter.

The battery doesn't charge below 32°F (0°C). In cold weather, bring it indoors to charge.

When hardwired, the doorbell trickle-charges the battery. If the battery keeps draining while hardwired, the transformer is probably under 10 VA; upgrade to a 16 VAC 30 VA transformer.

## Installation

Mount the doorbell about 48 inches (1.2 m) above the ground. Use the included 15° wedge if the door is in a corner. For hardwired installs, turn off power at the breaker first and connect the two existing doorbell wires to the terminals on the back (polarity doesn't matter).

If you have a mechanical chime, install the included Chime Kit at the chime box. Digital/electronic chimes aren't supported; use the plug-in Kestrel Chime instead.

## Setup

1. Charge the battery fully before setup.
2. In the Kestrel Home app tap Add Device > Doorbell and scan the QR code on the back.
3. Press the orange setup button on the back. The light ring spins white.
4. Connect to Wi-Fi. Stand next to the door during setup to check signal strength at the install location.

## Light ring

- Spinning white: setup mode.
- Solid blue ring: button pressed / live view active.
- Flashing blue: connecting to Wi-Fi.
- Flashing red: Wi-Fi connection failed.
- Solid red for 3 seconds then off: low battery, below 10%.

## Motion detection

Adjust motion zones and sensitivity in Device Settings > Motion. Person detection filters out cars and animals. Use "Motion schedule" to turn motion alerts off at set times. Reducing sensitivity and limiting zones is the best way to extend battery life.

## Troubleshooting

**Video is choppy or live view won't load.** Run Device Health in the app. If the RSSI is weaker than -65 dBm, add a Wi-Fi extender or the Kestrel Chime (which also works as a Wi-Fi bridge). Upload speed must be at least 2 Mbps.

**Chime doesn't ring.** Check the Chime Kit is installed and that "Mechanical chime" is enabled in Device Settings > Chime. If your chime is digital, it isn't supported.

**Battery drains in under a month.** Too many motion events. Narrow the motion zones, turn on person-only alerts, and disable "Pre-roll" which keeps the camera partially awake.

**Night video is washed out.** Something reflective near the lens (a wall or railing) bounces the infrared light. Adjust the angle with the wedge or turn on "IR reflection reduction."

## Reset

Press and hold the orange setup button for 20 seconds until the light ring flashes white. Your recordings stay in the cloud, but you'll need to set the doorbell up again.

## Warranty

1 year limited warranty. Theft protection: Kestrel replaces stolen doorbells for free with a police report.
