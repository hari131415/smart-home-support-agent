# Kestrel Indoor Camera C1 User Manual

## Specifications

- Video: 2K (2304x1296), 130° field of view, night vision up to 30 feet
- Pan and tilt: 360° pan, 90° tilt
- Audio: two-way talk with noise cancellation
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz only
- Local storage: microSD card up to 256 GB, formatted as FAT32 or exFAT
- Power: 5V/1A USB-C, indoor use only

## Setup

1. Plug in the camera. Wait for the status light to blink amber (about 30 seconds).
2. In the Kestrel Home app tap Add Device > Camera.
3. Choose your 2.4 GHz network. The app shows a QR code; hold your phone 6 to 8 inches in front of the lens until the camera chimes.

## Status light

- Blinking amber: ready for setup.
- Blinking blue: connecting.
- Solid blue: connected.
- Solid red: can't connect to Wi-Fi or the internet.
- Light off: privacy mode or status light turned off in settings.

## Privacy mode

Privacy mode turns the lens toward the body and stops recording and streaming. Turn it on from the app, or set it to activate automatically when you arrive home using Geofencing.

## Recording and storage

With a microSD card, the camera records motion events locally. Turn on Continuous Recording to record 24/7; a 128 GB card holds about 10 days of 2K continuous video. When the card fills, the oldest footage is overwritten.

Cloud recording requires a Kestrel Protect plan and keeps 30 days of events.

## Troubleshooting

**Camera shows offline.** Check the status light. Solid red means it can't reach Wi-Fi: restart your router, then unplug the camera for 10 seconds. Remember the C1 works on 2.4 GHz only.

**microSD card not detected.** Use a card of 256 GB or less, then format it in the app (Device Settings > Storage > Format). Cards formatted as NTFS won't work.

**Video lagging or pixelated.** Lower the resolution to 1080p in Device Settings > Video quality, or move the camera closer to the router. Each camera needs about 1.5 Mbps upload for 2K.

**Too many motion alerts.** Use Activity Zones to ignore areas like windows or a TV, and turn on Person detection.

**Camera won't pan.** Make sure nothing is blocking the camera head and that it's on a flat surface. Run Pan calibration from Device Settings > Advanced.

## Reset

Press and hold the reset button next to the microSD slot for 10 seconds until you hear a chime. Local recordings on the microSD card are not erased.
