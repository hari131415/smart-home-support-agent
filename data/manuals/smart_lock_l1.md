# Kestrel Smart Lock L1 User Manual

## Specifications

- Fits standard deadbolts on doors 1-3/8" to 2-1/4" thick
- Power: 4 AA alkaline batteries. Don't use rechargeable or lithium batteries.
- Battery life: about 6 months with 10 lock/unlock events per day
- Connectivity: Bluetooth Low Energy to phone; Thread via the Kestrel Hub H1 for remote access
- Keypad: up to 250 access codes, 4 to 8 digits
- Rating: ANSI/BHMA Grade 2

## Installation

The L1 replaces the interior side of your existing deadbolt and keeps your exterior keyway, so your keys still work. Before installing, make sure the deadbolt throws and retracts smoothly by hand with the door closed. If you have to push or pull the door to turn the key, adjust the strike plate first; the motor can't overcome a binding bolt.

## Setup and calibration

1. Insert the 4 AA batteries. The lock beeps twice.
2. In the Kestrel Home app tap Add Device > Lock and follow the prompts.
3. During calibration, the app asks you to lock and unlock by hand so the lock learns your door's direction.
4. To control the lock away from home, add it to a Kestrel Hub H1 within 30 feet (9 m).

## Access codes

Create codes in the app under Lock > Access Codes. Codes can be permanent, scheduled (certain days and hours), or one-time. After 5 wrong code entries in a row the keypad locks out for 60 seconds.

## Auto-lock and DoorSense

Auto-lock relocks the door after a delay you set from 30 seconds to 30 minutes. DoorSense uses the included magnet on the door frame to know whether the door is open, so auto-lock won't throw the bolt into an open door. Re-run DoorSense calibration after moving the magnet.

## Battery

The app shows battery level. You'll get a low battery alert at 20%. At 5% the lock still works but the keypad light flashes red on every use.

If the batteries die completely, use your physical key. Alternatively, touch a 9V battery to the two contacts under the keypad to power it temporarily, then enter your code.

## Troubleshooting

**Lock jams or doesn't fully extend.** Most jams are door alignment, not the lock. Open the door and test the lock: if it works smoothly with the door open, the strike plate needs adjusting. Re-run calibration from Lock Settings > Calibrate.

**Shows offline / no remote access.** Remote access goes through the Hub H1. Check the hub is online and within 30 feet. Move the hub closer, or add a mains-powered Thread device such as a Kestrel Color Bulb B2 between them to act as a repeater. (The P1 plug is Wi-Fi only and won't extend the mesh.)

**Keypad doesn't respond.** Replace all 4 batteries at the same time. If it still doesn't respond, remove batteries for 30 seconds and reinsert.

**Auto-lock isn't working.** Check DoorSense is calibrated and auto-lock is enabled. Auto-lock pauses while the door is detected as open.

## Factory reset

Remove the battery cover and one battery. Press and hold the reset button inside the battery compartment while reinserting the battery, and keep holding for 5 seconds until it beeps 3 times. All access codes are erased.

## Warranty

Mechanical parts and finish: lifetime limited warranty. Electronics: 2 years.
