# Kestrel Smart Plug P1 User Manual

## Specifications

- Rated load: 15 A / 1800 W resistive at 120 VAC, 60 Hz
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz only
- Energy monitoring: real-time watts, daily and monthly kWh
- Indoor use only. Operating temperature 32°F to 104°F (0°C to 40°C).
- Do not use with space heaters, air conditioners above 15 A, or medical equipment.

## Setup

1. Plug the P1 into a wall outlet. The LED blinks blue quickly to show it is ready for setup.
2. In the Kestrel Home app tap Add Device > Smart Plug.
3. Choose your 2.4 GHz network and enter the password.
4. Keep your phone within 10 feet (3 m) of the plug during setup.

If the LED is not blinking blue quickly, press and hold the button for 5 seconds to enter setup mode.

## LED indicator

- Fast blinking blue: setup mode.
- Slow blinking blue: connecting to Wi-Fi.
- Solid blue: connected, power on.
- LED off: connected, power off (or LED disabled in settings).
- Blinking red: lost Wi-Fi connection.
- Solid red: overload protection tripped.

## Schedules, timers and away mode

Each plug supports up to 32 schedules. Countdown timers can be set from 1 minute to 24 hours. Away mode turns the plug on and off at random times between the start and end times you choose, to make the home look occupied.

Schedules are stored on the plug, so they keep running even if Wi-Fi goes down, as long as the plug has power. After a power outage the plug resyncs its clock when Wi-Fi returns.

## Power-on behavior

Under Settings > Power-on state, choose what happens after a power outage: Off (default), On, or Last state.

## Energy monitoring

The app shows live wattage and history. Readings are accurate to within ±2% above 5 W. Loads under 1 W may show as 0 W.

## Troubleshooting

**Plug won't connect during setup.** Make sure you're on a 2.4 GHz network and that the Wi-Fi password is correct. Networks with a captive portal (hotel or dorm Wi-Fi) aren't supported. Temporarily disable "band steering" on mesh routers, or create a separate 2.4 GHz SSID.

**LED blinking red.** The plug lost Wi-Fi. Check the router, then unplug the P1 for 10 seconds and plug it back in.

**Solid red LED / plug shut off.** Overload protection tripped because the connected device drew more than 15 A. Unplug the load, wait 30 seconds, then press the button to reset. Don't connect that device to the P1 again.

**Schedule ran at the wrong time.** Check the time zone in the app under Settings > Home > Time zone.

**Plug is warm.** Slight warmth under heavy load is normal. If it is too hot to touch, smells like burning, or the outlet is discolored, unplug it immediately and stop using it.

## Factory reset

Press and hold the button for 15 seconds until the LED blinks red and then fast blue. This erases schedules and Wi-Fi settings.
