# Kestrel Smoke & CO Alarm S1 User Manual

## Important safety information

If the alarm sounds for smoke or fire, get everyone out of the house immediately and call emergency services from outside. Don't try to troubleshoot the alarm.

If the carbon monoxide (CO) alarm sounds, move everyone to fresh air outdoors or by an open door or window, and call emergency services. CO is a colorless, odorless gas that can kill. Don't go back inside until emergency responders say it's safe.

This product is not a substitute for a properly installed and maintained fire alarm system.

## Specifications

- Sensors: photoelectric smoke sensor and electrochemical CO sensor
- Power: wired 120 VAC with 2 AA backup batteries, or battery-only model with 6 AA lithium batteries
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz; interconnects with other S1 alarms wirelessly
- Alarm volume: 85 dB at 10 feet
- Sensor lifespan: 10 years from the manufacture date printed on the back

## Installation

Install one alarm inside each bedroom, outside each sleeping area, and on every level of the home including the basement. Mount on the ceiling at least 4 inches from the wall, or on a wall 4 to 12 inches below the ceiling.

Don't install within 10 feet of a cooking appliance, near bathrooms with showers, or near fans and vents that could blow smoke away.

## Alarm and light patterns

- Green light once a minute: normal operation (can be turned off at night).
- Yellow light with spoken "Heads-up, there's smoke/CO": early warning, levels are rising.
- Red light with loud alarm: emergency. Smoke: 3 beeps repeating. CO: 4 beeps repeating.
- Single chirp every 60 seconds with yellow light: low battery.
- Yellow light with "Sensor expired" message: replace the alarm.

## Testing

Press the button and hold for 2 seconds to run a sound check. Use the Safety Checkup in the app monthly. The app also runs a silent self-test of the sensors every 30 days.

## Silencing

To silence a nuisance alarm (for example from cooking), press the button. Smoke alarms can be silenced for 60 seconds only if smoke levels are not very high. A CO alarm can't be silenced if CO levels are dangerous.

## Battery

Wired models: replace the 2 AA backup batteries when the low battery chirp starts or the app alerts you. Battery-only models: replace all 6 lithium AA batteries at once. The battery-only model lasts about 5 years per set.

## Troubleshooting

**Low battery chirp.** Replace the batteries. On wired models the chirp means the backup batteries are low even if the alarm has AC power.

**Nuisance alarms from cooking or steam.** Move the alarm further from the kitchen or bathroom (at least 10 feet).

**Alarm shows offline in the app.** It still protects your home locally. Check Wi-Fi, then press the button once to wake it and reconnect.

**"Sensor expired."** The alarm has reached its 10 year life. Replace it; it can't be repaired.
